**F10 (a), FAIL: a hidden door face can light through a thin surface.** The column is marked when the depth reading lies within `[pred - (2.0 + 0.05 * pred), pred + 1.5]` levels of the predicted face depth (`env/door_cue.py:59-64, 108-109`). Converted with the module's own fit (`DEPTH_PER_UNIT = 0.13979`) and measured by calling `door_channel` with a flat synthetic reading placed in front of a face straight ahead:

| Face distance (map units) | Lights with an occluding surface up to this far in front of the face |
|---|---|
| 64 | 16 units |
| 128 | 23 units |
| 256 | 29 units |
| 400 | 30 units |

So a wall, pillar or other world surface up to 16-30 units in front of a door face that is not visible produces a door cue on those columns. This contradicts the module's own contract ("A door behind a wall ... never lights", `env/door_cue.py:24-25`) and RULES "no god-view radar" in that band. The engine test for a hidden door (`tests/test_env_obs_v2_engine.py:125-190`) covers thick occluders only. Limits of this finding: it was shown on the pure function, not in the engine; whether geometry with a surface that close in front of a door face occurs on the generated maps or on vanilla maps was **not measured**. The clearance involved is narrower than the player (32 units), so the practical gain is small, but the code as written reveals the door.

**F10 (b), UNCLEAR: door identity is engine truth, not appearance.** `DoorTracker.faces_now` registers every sector of the whole level whose ceiling equals its floor (`env/door_cue.py:138-153`), whether or not the player has seen it; the docstring's "seen closed" means "present in the sector list", not "seen by the player". Only the output is line-of-sight gated. Consequence: a zero-height sector that does not look like a door (a secret door textured as wall, a monster closet) lights when its face is in view. The surface is in the field of view, but its nature is not perceivable by a player. `engine-probes.md:358` records that non-door zero-gap sectors on vanilla maps were not surveyed. The brief recommends "extracting internal sector geometry", so this may be acceptable; it is a judgement call for `advisor` and a question for the organizers.

**J1. Is spawn-relative odometry from engine position acceptable ego-centric sensing?** My reading: yes under the rules as written, with a question for the organizers. Reasons: the brief names "local coordinate frame transformations" and occupancy grids as intended techniques; nothing in the output identifies a map location (verified numerically above); the grid holds only what the depth sensor saw and where the agent's body went; no path, goal direction or frontier is computed, so no action is pre-computed (rule 4.1). What a strict reader could object to: the pose comes from privileged engine variables with zero drift (a player has no such sense), and the memory is hand-written rather than learned. Neither is prohibited by the ground rules, but both depend on the same engine variables that `+viz_nocheat 1` removes (question Q1).

## 5. Questions only the organizers can settle

- **Q1.** Will the evaluation harness set `+viz_nocheat 1`, or otherwise restrict the depth buffer, labels, game variables (position, angle, velocity) or sector info? Every spatial input of the agent depends on them.
- **Q2.** Is reading the engine's sector list at evaluation acceptable when it is used only to mark, in line of sight, that a visible surface is a closed door? This includes surfaces that do not look like doors.
- **Q3.** Is exact pose from engine variables acceptable as odometry for an agent-centred memory map, given that no coordinate is output?
- **Q4.** Is a scripted teacher (pathfinding and aiming) acceptable as a training-time source of demonstrations, given that the final agent's actions come only from the trained network? (Open in `CLAUDE.md` section 7.)
- **Q5.** Is sampling from the network's action distribution at evaluation acceptable, or must the agent be deterministic?
- **Q6.** Which IWAD will the judges use? Key-pickup and locked-door wording for the Doom IWADs is taken from the string table and has not been probed.
