# BC student fails in closed loop: diagnosis and DAgger (2026-11-25)

Authorised by CLAUDE.md s7, 2026-11-25 (evening) plan change. Debugger task. Nothing
committed. `heldout_maps/` and `--split heldout` were not touched.

## 1. Diagnosis

### (a) Train/eval mismatch or plumbing bug: NO plumbing bug; ONE input-distribution mismatch (attack bit)

* Env config: the generated-maps TRAIN config the dataset was collected with and the
  EVAL config differ only in host/harness fields (`name`, `vanilla_eval`,
  `win_timer_ms`, `maps`, `episode_timeout_tics`, `map_wad`); sensor signatures equal.
* Bit-exact re-creation: 4 dataset episodes (S0 x2, S1, one noisy) were re-played in a
  fresh env (same map, seed, executed actions). Every observation key of every step
  equals the saved one (max |diff| = 0.0); all 4 reach the exit again.
* Recurrent plumbing: 5 dataset episodes run step by step through
  `agent.inference.PolicyRunner`-style `model.step` (fresh state, `episode_start` 1 at
  t = 0) vs `forward_sequence` as `train/bc.py` uses it: max |logit difference|
  3.8e-6 to 6.2e-6 (float noise). Step-0 argmax actions in the env equal the dataset's.
* The mismatch: the attack head had loss weight 0, so it stays ~50/50 (argmax picks
  "fire" on 89-94 % of decisions). The executed action is fed back as
  `obs["prev_action"]`, and `prev_action[attack] = 1` NEVER occurs in `bc_v1` (the
  teacher never fires and noise bursts force attack 0). Offline, setting that bit at
  random on 50 % of steps of the BC-val episodes drops accuracy move 0.919 -> 0.904,
  turn 0.677 -> 0.619. In closed loop the effect compounds (see (b)).

### (b) Closed loop on TRAIN maps (60-epoch model, shadow teacher; 4 maps x 2 episodes per stage, train env)

Ranges are over episodes (1,050-decision time-outs unless successful). "Still" = share
of decisions that moved the player < 2 units.

| variant | episodes (S0+S1) | success | forward pressed | still | zero-turn chosen | USE 0->1 edges / ep | L/R sign flips |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sample (as `evaluate --sample`) | 8+3 | 0 | 0.91-0.97 | 0.74-0.91 | 0.58-0.67 | 4-7 | 0.48-0.61 |
| sample, attack forced 0 after the net (diagnostic only) | 8+4 | 0 | 0.69-0.75 | 0.37-0.51 | 0.48-0.59 | 43-54 | 0.24-0.30 |
| argmax | 8+4 | 0 | 0.96-0.99 | 0.86-0.96 | 0.85-0.95 | 0-1 | 0.21-0.53 |
| argmax, attack forced 0 (diagnostic only) | 4+4 | 1 (S0) | 0.72-0.81 | 0.25-0.66 | 0.42-0.76 | 5-39 | 0.14-0.21 |

* Typical failure: the student presses FORWARD into a wall and keeps pressing (91-99 %
  of decisions forward while 74-96 % make no progress), and chooses zero-turn far more
  often than the teacher does (the teacher's labels are zero-turn on 23 % of steps).
  It does not spin; it is pinned against geometry.
* With the untrained attack head the model fires on ~50 % (sample) / ~92 % (argmax)
  of decisions, and then almost never turns (zero-turn 0.85-0.95 under argmax): the
  `prev_action` attack bit pushes it off its training distribution. Firing itself
  does not alter the rays/entity rows (weapon sprite, firing overlay and puffs are
  already excluded, CLAUDE.md s8); there are no monsters on S0-S2 to alert.
* Forcing attack 0 roughly halves stand-still and restores USE presses, but success
  stays near 0: the attack bit is one cause, not the only one.
* Fix of the attack head (train it toward label 0 with full weight, 5 epochs from the
  60-epoch model): see the result table (row "BC 60 + attack head").

### (c) Teacher agreement along the student's own trajectory

The teacher controller ran in shadow on the student's states (re-targeting from any
position). Argmax student with attack forced 0, from the same start state as dataset
episode 0 (S0-train-100001): move, strafe and use agree with the teacher through
decision 27 (turn already differs by one bin from decision 7, speed at 11 and 16; the
dataset path is followed approximately), then at decision 28 the student stops at a
corner where the teacher keeps moving (P(move none) 0.55), and agreement collapses:
share of decisions with ALL heads equal to the teacher, per 50-decision window:
0.34 / 0.16 / 0.08 / 0.00 / 0.04 ... (sampled student: 0.04 / 0.00 / 0.00 ...).
Per-head agreement over whole episodes: move 0.17-0.50, turn 0.24-0.55, strafe
0.29-0.83, use 0.95-1.0, speed 0.94-1.0. Disagreement is concentrated in
"approach" phases (pursuing the next door/exit), i.e. in states where the student is
pressed against a wall or facing away from the route, which the teacher's own
trajectories (even with 20 % noise bursts) never contain.
Note: in this first shadow run the teacher's scripted unstick routine fired 42-117
times per episode (its stuck test saw the student pushing a wall); those steps carry
back/strafe labels that do not depend on the observation, so they deflate the
agreement numbers and would poison DAgger labels. Fixed before collection (s2).
With the gated teacher (s2), length-weighted agreement on the 120 TRAIN-map
measurement episodes, all heads / move / turn: BC 60 + attack 0.054 / 0.25 / 0.11;
DAgger r1 0.159 / 0.54 / 0.29; DAgger r3 0.277 / 0.70 / 0.36. Agreement on the
student's own states keeps rising each round even where success does not (s3).

### (d) Label ambiguity of the turn head (60-epoch model, BC-val maps, teacher states, 43,503 steps)

Turn accuracy 0.674 (train-split 0.732: the model cannot fit the training maps either).
Of the 14,192 turn errors: 73 % are an ADJACENT bin (quantisation of the teacher's
exact atan2 bearing to a look-ahead point it alone can see; low consequence), 43 %
are turn vs no-turn, 40 % same direction wrong size, and 16.5 % are the OPPOSITE
direction (2,347 steps = 5.4 % of all steps; 643 of them are full -10 vs +10 reversals,
1.5 % of steps). So at most ~5 % of decisions are plausibly "route not inferable from
the observation" (branch choice); most of the 33 % is fine-grained bin noise. This is
not what breaks the closed loop: the dataset path is followed approximately until the
first off-distribution state (c).

**Verdict:** no plumbing bug. Closed-loop failure = (1) an untrained attack head whose
executed output feeds an input the model never saw (fixed by training it), and (2)
classic compounding error: off-route/against-wall states are absent from the teacher
data, so the student has no correction there (fixed by DAgger).

## 2. Changes

Training-side only. `agent/`, `env/`, `evaluate/` untouched; no override, mask or
scripted fallback at inference.

* `teachers/controller.py` (map-forge/worker teacher code): `ControllerConfig.student_driven`
  (DAgger labelling). When the student drives: an executed action that differs from
  the label is not treated as a noise burst (stuck history kept); the scripted unstick
  is entered only when the teacher's own steering label is "forward" and the executed
  forward made no progress (otherwise the label is the steering correction, e.g. turn
  toward the route); the unstick strafe side is the side with more free grid cells
  (`_freer_side`), not a coin flip. `label_source` ("nav" / "unstick") per label.
  Default (`False`) leaves teacher-driven collection unchanged.
  Measured: unstick labels fell from 42-117 per 1,050-decision episode (shadow run,
  ungated) to 0.02 % of DAgger labels.
* `teachers/collect.py`: DAgger mode `--student CKPT [--beta b] [--max-steps N]
  [--student-argmax]`. The student (`StudentDriver`: checkpoint -> `agent.inference.
  PolicyRunner`, sampled, CPU, 1 torch thread per worker) drives; with probability b
  per decision the teacher's label is executed instead; the label is always the
  teacher's (re-targeted from wherever the student is). Same dataset contract plus
  `label_unstick` and `student_driven` arrays; every DAgger episode is saved; capped
  episodes are not successes; `summary.json` gets a `dagger` block (success by stage,
  unstick share, agreement). `--student --no-save` with b = 0 measures closed-loop
  success on TRAIN maps.
* `train/bc.py` (worker): `--data` repeatable and comma-separated (union of dirs);
  `--init-from-bc PATH` starts from a previous BC model (strict; refuses a different
  sensor signature / model config / obs spec) and keeps that model's BC-val map ids as
  the val split, so maps held out of cloning stay held out across rounds.
* Tests: `tests/test_teachers_controller.py` (+4: gated unstick and free side,
  stuck history kept under override, DAgger episode contract / beta 0 and 1 / cap, CLI
  checks; contract test extended with the two new arrays), `tests/test_bc.py` (+2:
  several data dirs + `--init-from-bc` keeps val ids and weights; model-config refusal).
* Attack head: every model from "BC 60 + attack head" on is trained with
  `--head-weights 1,1,1,1,1,1` (label always 0 on S0-S2). The default in
  `configs/bc_config.py` is unchanged (still 0); combat data must revisit this.

Measurement notes: val numbers come from `python -m evaluate.run --checkpoint <m>
--split val --episodes-per-map 3 --sample --seed 11` (vanilla EVAL config, same seeds
for every model). The runs made here used `DOOMSDAY_WIN_TIMER_MS=1` (host timer only;
the default EVAL run waits on the 15.6 ms Windows tick, ~14 decisions/s here); the
BC 20 and random-policy val rows are the earlier runs without it.
`teachers/` has no owner in the subagent ownership list (it is the 2026-11-25 evening
plan's teacher code); whoever owns it should know the controller changed.
The teacher's `_recover` (off-field / in a doorway) still labels "back" (with a
random-side strafe off the door cells); measured share of MOVE_BACK labels in the
DAgger data: 1.1 % / 3.1 % / 0.2 % / 0.9 % (r1, r1b, r2, r3), almost all from
`_recover`, not from unstick (0.0-0.02 %). TRAIN-map numbers
come from `python -m teachers.collect --stages S0 S1 S2 --episodes-per-map 2
--student <m> --no-save --seed 777` (train env, 60 TRAIN maps x 2 episodes, same seeds
for every model, sampled actions, full 1,050-decision limit). The 60 TRAIN maps include
the 6 BC-val maps (S1-train-101005/6/8/12, S2-train-102005/19) that no model was cloned
on; the DAgger collection also visits them, but `--init-from-bc` keeps them out of
every training split.

## 4. What is still wrong

1. **Doors (S1/S2): DAgger did not help, and the cause is in the teacher's labels.** In
   the round-1 TRAIN measurement, 35 of 38 failed S1 episodes ended in the teacher's
   door "approach" phase at the first door. Across their last 40 controller events the
   teacher entered "align" 89 times, but "press" only 10 times and "push" 5 times. The
   teacher labels USE only after the player stands at a hidden stand-off point AND
   faces the door within 6 degrees. Until then the label is "turn" or "approach". The
   student's coarse turning seldom satisfies this gate, so USE labels are 0.9 % of
   steps in student-driven S1 data (196 of 22,479). Suggested fix for the teacher owner
   (training-time only): label USE whenever the engine itself would open the door (door
   line within USE range and under the crosshair), so the label depends on what the
   student can see. Then collect a fresh dataset, because mixing old and new USE labels
   would conflict.
2. **Turn precision.** Turn accuracy is 0.67-0.74 even on cloned maps; 73 % of turn
   errors are adjacent bins. This comes from the teacher's atan2 aim at a look-ahead
   point the student cannot see. Coarser or smoothed turn labels would make the target
   learnable.
0. **Not better than pure PPO.** The PPO checkpoint from earlier today reached ~30-60 % on
   S0 TRAIN maps and ~5 % on S1; DAgger round 1 (45 % S0, 5 % S1 TRAIN) is on par, not
   better. BC + DAgger is a starting point for PPO, not a replacement.
3. **Plateau after one round.** More student-state data (up to about 1M steps) with
   union training did not lift S0 beyond round 1 (rounds 2-3 within noise, nominally
   lower), although teacher agreement on student states kept rising. Possible causes,
   not tested: imbalance between teacher-state and DAgger
   data, too few epochs per round, or the model's capacity on turn. The planned next
   step (PPO fine-tune from the round-1 model) is the natural place to fix the
   remaining S0 failures.
4. **Attack head.** It is now trained toward 0 with full weight. Its entropy is gone
   (accuracy 1.000), so PPO must re-explore it; `configs/bc_config.py` still defaults
   to weight 0. The combat stage must decide how to handle this.
5. **Default EVAL runs are slow on this laptop** (~14 decisions/s on the 15.6 ms
   Windows tick); `DOOMSDAY_WIN_TIMER_MS=1` (host setting, no rule change) made the val
   runs here ~5x faster.

## 5. Reproduce

```
# round k (k >= 1), previous model M, round dirs D1..Dk
python -m teachers.collect --stages S0 S1 S2 --episodes-per-map 9 --student M --beta 0.1 \
    --max-steps 400 --out data/teacher/dagger_rk --workers 2 --seed <k>01
python -m train.bc --data data/teacher/bc_v1,D1,...,Dk --init-from-bc M --out reports/runs/<dir> \
    --epochs 8 --device cuda --seed 1 --head-weights 1,1,1,1,1,1
python -m teachers.collect --stages S0 S1 S2 --episodes-per-map 2 --student <new> --no-save \
    --out reports/eval/2026-11-25-bc-dagger/train_<name> --workers 2 --seed 777
DOOMSDAY_WIN_TIMER_MS=1 python -m evaluate.run --checkpoint <new> --split val --episodes-per-map 3 \
    --sample --seed 11 --out reports/eval/2026-11-25-bc-dagger/val_<name>
```
